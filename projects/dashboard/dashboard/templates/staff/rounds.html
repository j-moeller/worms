Rounds

A round is defined here: its window, its gameplan and the duration per team. Nothing is built until it starts. Then the conductor runs every team's worm, one after another: the round agent on the Proxmox host builds the gameplan's sandbox once and rolls it back for each team, and the gameplan's execute() plays out. An attempt the infrastructure fails is thrown away and run again.
