Submit flags and logs

From inside the sandbox, over the internal network. No token or team id: only one team runs at a time, so the server knows which. Capturing a VM means submitting its flag (/etc/worms/flag on each VM). The full contract for your worm is docs/worm-contract.md.

curl -X POST https://scoring.worms.internal/submit \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"vm_flag":"FLAG"}'
curl -X POST https://logging.worms.internal/submit \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"log_data":{"event":"..."}}'

Download your results

Your scoring and logs for a round as JSON. The token goes in the header, never the URL. Pages hold up to 500 submissions or 200 logs (1 MiB); follow page.next until it is null. Over the rate limit you get HTTP 429.

TOKEN=...   # a token of your team
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" {{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}/api/v1/rounds/
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" "{{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}/api/v1/rounds/ID/scoring/?limit=500"
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" "{{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}/api/v1/rounds/ID/logging/?limit=200&cursor=NEXT"
